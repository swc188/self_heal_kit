> **One-liner**: By submitting code or docs to `self_heal_kit`, you agree to license your contribution **sublicensably** to the project, enabling dual licensing under GPLv3+ and commercial terms. This is the legal prerequisite for the dual-license model — without it, any external contribution would prevent the project from relicensing unilaterally.

This CLA applies to **any** contribution you submit to the project (code, docs, tests, config, text explicitly offered as a patch in Issues, etc.), but not to mere reading, usage, or bug reporting.

- **"Contribution"**: Original content and modifications you intentionally submit for inclusion via Pull Request, patch, commit, email, or any project-accepted channel.
- **"Project"**: `self_heal_kit` and derivative versions released by maintainers.

## 8. Honest Boundaries (Current Limitations)

This is a **lightweight "contribution = agreement" CLA** with no independent signing archive, suitable for early-stage, few-contributor phase. If any of the following arise, upgrade to formal **ICLA / CCLA + signing archive** (e.g., CLA Assistant, record signer identity & timestamp):

- Accepting large-scale corporate employee contributions;
- Commercial license contracts with significant value requiring full copyright chain proof to clients;
- Need to provide rights provenance evidence to third parties (acquirers, auditors, compliance certifiers).

**Until then, do not treat this CLA as "completed copyright due diligence".**
